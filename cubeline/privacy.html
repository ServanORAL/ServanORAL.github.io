Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cubeline game (“the app”) for Android and iOS handles and why. The app is developed by Servan Oral (“we”), who is the data controller.

No account, no server of ours

You do not create an account and we do not ask for your name or email address. We do not run a server for the app, so your games are never sent to us.

Stored on your device

Your best score, your chosen language and your vibration setting are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you remove the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads are personalized only where you have allowed it.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch you are asked for consent through Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices (ads).
  • iPhone: the app asks for permission to track. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared and you see non-personalized ads.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Privacy → Ads).

See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ad-free purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. To check whether ad-free is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random app identifier and your purchase history (products, dates, status). See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Legal basis

Personalized ads are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalized ads are based on our legitimate interest in funding the free game. Checking your purchases is necessary to provide what you bought.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to withdraw your consent. We do not hold your data ourselves; for data processed by Google or RevenueCat you can contact us or them directly. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
